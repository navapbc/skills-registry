## Database

All goals and metrics are tracked in a single Confluence database (ID: 2405662852) with different filtered views for company goals, team build goals, and health metrics.

## Database Columns

- **Goal and Success Criteria** — The outcome we are working toward for a company goal, team build goal, or team health metric
- **Type** — Indicates whether the item is a company goal, team build goal, or company health metric
- **Core Operational Area** — Where the essential day-to-day work happens at Nava (Growth, Delivery, Talent, Operations, Finance, Market Shaping)
- **Driver** — The individual actively ensuring progress toward the outcome
- **Approver** — The individual ultimately accountable for the outcome
- **Tags** — Used to filter and identify overlap across departments, capabilities, categories (evolving list)
- **Plain Language "Why"** — Why the outcome matters and what will be important for success
- **Which Strategic Plan?** — The departments this goal or metric is most closely aligned with
- **Monthly Updates** — Rolling monthly progress columns (e.g., "Jan 26", "Feb 26")
