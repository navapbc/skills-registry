## Overview

In 2026, Nava is entering the second year of a two-year focus to be the premier **human-centered integrator** in the US mid-market by 2027.

A "human-centered integrator" is a company that brings together separate products or subsystems into one unified, holistic system and ensures those sub-components function together well — while doing so in a way that keeps things simple, effective, and accessible for the people who depend on it (end users, government staff, and other stakeholders).

## Structure

Supporting the 6 top-level company goals are:
- **29 team build goals** — creating, launching, or building something new, or evolving current practices significantly
- **26 company health metrics** — evergreen measures of business and operational health

## Goal 1: Growth — Win the most consequential prime contracts at a price premium

We are building the most impressive portfolio of work that enables us to improve millions of lives, improve trust in public institutions, and prove to clients that we are the least risky vendor to hire based on track record. Our body of work spans important services, programs, and agencies that serve as reference examples to influence how future clients think and act.

**How everyone contributes:** Pitch in on a bid. Develop and pitch organic growth ideas with your team that add real value for users and the agency.

---

## Goal 2: Delivery — Beat client expectations consistently as a human-centered integrator

We deliver shockingly great outcomes faster than clients expect to set standards for other vendors. We deliver strong client service with every interaction. We are the trusted advisors who bring the best of custom and commercial together to meet the needs of users and clients in the immediate-term, while helping the government make sound choices in a noisy technology landscape long-term.

**Key metric (from Rohan's 2026 goals huddle):** Legacy lockpicks migrate 10 complex, in-production subsystems to modern tech stacks on new, organically grown, or existing contracts.

**How everyone contributes:** Act as a Trusted Advisor to clients, live our values and find little ways to introduce positive surprises. Embrace a growth mindset to reflect, take feedback, improve. Build your client services skills and consulting skills. Contribute to broadening and improving our capabilities. Contribute to building Strata or Legacy Lockpicks, deploying them on contracts where they add value.

---

## Goal 3: Market Shaping — Convert more traditional client demand "in-market" to a human-centered delivery approach

We grow the size and density of the forward-thinking government client community, advance their careers, and authentically position Nava as most trusted. We positively influence traditional client types (OCIO, program leaders, etc.) to structure RFPs to use a human-centered delivery approach. We fearlessly and publicly push for the adoption and spread of healthy standards and practices.

**How everyone contributes:** Write, speak, attend events, be an authentic and effective ambassador for Nava and what we stand for.

---

## Goal 4: Talent — Field the industry's most effective teams

We rapidly build high-performing, outcome-oriented teams, drawing from a broad and deep community of cultivated talent, experts, and consultants. We intentionally build deep domain expertise and valuable institutional knowledge in people, teams, and as a company. We practice and live our culture of high trust, effectiveness, learning, and proactive evolution.

**How everyone contributes:** Operate urgently, cohesively, creatively. Interview the next generation of talented Navanauts. Refer the most talented folks you know. Onboard new teammates well. Give feedback that is clear and kind; hold teammates and reports accountable for high standards. Take feedback as a gift, as an opportunity to improve and grow, even if it's hard to hear.

---

## Goal 5: Operations — Center efficient, scalable operations on delivery success

We design, run, and adapt our ops to be efficient, scalable, and enable all delivery teams to thrive even as their needs change. We build systems and structures to cultivate cohesion and build genuine institutional knowledge by making knowledge, data, insight, and learning from across the company's entire surface area easily accessible to the people who need it.

**How everyone contributes:** Operate urgently, cohesively, creatively using a human-centered approach. Make data, policies, knowledge, information easily accessible. Identify where you and your team can work smarter. Build processes and tools that make it better and faster for the next person. Give ops teams useful feedback to triage and act on.

---

## Goal 6: Finance — Invest to maximize durable mid-market competitive advantages

We build and maintain a financial posture to price competitively, invest strategically, profit sufficiently, and operate resiliently. We asymmetrically tap philanthropic capital to defray the costs of high leverage R&D and growth efforts.

**How everyone contributes:** Achieve your annual hours floor. Submit accurate timesheets on time. Contribute to initiatives across our six goals that increase Nava's differentiation and ability to command a durable price premium in the mid-market.

## Goal Flywheel

The 6 goals interleave and reinforce one another. When spinning as a flywheel, they have Nava running and then leaping towards being the premier human-centered integrator in US markets.
